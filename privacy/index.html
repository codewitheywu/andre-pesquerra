Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Overview

This site (andre-pesquerra.vercel.app, also mirrored on GitHub Pages) is Andre V. Pesquerra's personal portfolio. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, how it's used, and the choices you have.

Information Collected

Contact form. If you submit the contact form, the name, email address, subject, and message you enter are stored, along with your IP address, which is used only to limit how many inquiries can be sent from one address per month (spam prevention). This data is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not sold or shared with third parties.

No accounts, no tracking pixels, no ad trackers. This site doesn't require an account, and doesn't run advertising trackers of any kind.

Cookies

A small amount of browser storage (a cookie or localStorage entry) remembers your cookie-consent choice so the banner doesn't reappear on every visit. If analytics is enabled (see below), it does not use tracking cookies. No other cookies are set by this site.

Third-Party Services

Translation. The "Translate to English" button on testimonials sends the testimonial text (already publicly displayed on this page) to the MyMemory Translation API, server-side. No personal visitor data is sent as part of that request.

Analytics. Vercel Web Analytics may be used to understand aggregate traffic (e.g. page views, referring sites). It's cookie-free and does not collect personally identifiable information.

Data Retention & Your Rights

Contact form submissions are retained so past inquiries can be referenced. You can request that your submitted data be deleted or corrected at any time by emailing the address below.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally to reflect changes to the site. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.
