Background music
================
The music dock (bottom-right of the home page) plays assets/audio/coffee.mp3.

- The dock is hidden until that file exists, so the site works fine without it.
- It never autoplays and plays quietly (see VOLUME in assets/js/main.js).
- Only add audio you have the right to publish. Hosting a commercial recording
  on a public site without a licence can trigger takedown notices against the
  GitHub repo and the Vercel deployment.
