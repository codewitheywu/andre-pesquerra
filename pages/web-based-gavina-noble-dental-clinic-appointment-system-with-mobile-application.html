Overview

🌱 The Beginning — Foundation & Planning
This all started with my Capstone/Thesis project: a full-stack Dental Clinic Management System for Gavina Noble Dental Clinic. My goal was to digitize and streamline the clinic's operations—from patient registration to appointments, records, billing, and more.
I designed a 19-table MySQL database schema from the ground up, covering patients, staff, appointments, treatments, inventory, and more. I built it using PHP + PDO + MySQL, with a clean blue/green color palette, Poppins + Roboto fonts, and a fully responsive UI.

🏗️ What I Built — Module by Module
👤 Patient-Facing Side

Landing Page — The public-facing intro to Gavina Noble Dental
Multi-step Patient Registration — A polished, step-by-step signup flow
Patient Login & Portal — Patients could log in, view their appointments, treatment history, and records
PWA / APK Install Option — Patients could install the system as an app on their phones

🔐 Role-Based Access Control
I implemented three distinct roles, each with their own dashboard and permissions:

Admin — Full control over everything
Doctor — Patient records, treatment plans, appointments
Receptionist — Appointment management, patient check-ins

📅 Appointment Management
Scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation
Auto-decline logic — Appointments were automatically declined after a set window
Notifications for patients and staff when appointment statuses changed

🦷 Clinical Modules
Treatment Plans — Doctors could create and manage multi-step treatment plans per patient
Medical Records — Full patient history, diagnoses, and notes
Services Management — The clinic's list of dental services with pricing

📦 Inventory System
Categories and items management
Inventory Transactions — Tracking stock in/out
Low-stock visibility for the admin

📊 Reports Module
Built with Chart.js for visual data
Export to PDF via jsPDF and Excel via SheetJS
Appointment stats, revenue summaries, and more

💬 Communication
Live Chat Widget — In-app messaging between staff and patients
Notifications System — Real-time alerts for appointments, messages, and system events

📱 Mobile Responsiveness
I refined the UI extensively across many breakpoints
Hamburger menus, consistent modal systems, sidebar navigation
Every page tested and polished for mobile

🎓 Research Side
Alongside the system, I also worked on the academic requirements:

Patient Satisfaction Questionnaires — Pre-test and post-test, using a 4-point Likert scale
APA Citations in Mondriaan Aura College's modified format
Conclusions and Recommendations for the research study

🚀 Deployment & Production

I hosted it on Hostinger at gavinadental.online
Configured clean URL routing via .htaccess (no index.php in URLs)
Set up subdomain configuration on Hostinger's control panel
Packaged it as an Android APK via Android Studio using a WebView wrapper
Fully deployed to production — live and accessible to real users

🏆 The Result
My Gavina Noble Dental Clinic Management System earned the 🥇 Best in Project Implementation award at my thesis defense at Mondriaan Aura College. It became the centerpiece of my portfolio and the proudest milestone of my capstone/thesis journey.

Challenges

🛠️ Technical Challenges
Building the system from scratch was no easy feat. One of the biggest hurdles was designing a 19-table relational database schema that could handle all the clinic's interconnected data—patients, appointments, treatments, inventory, and staff—without breaking relationships or causing redundancy.
Implementing role-based access control for three different user types (Admin, Doctor, Receptionist) meant I had to carefully think through every route, every page, and every action to make sure no one could access what they shouldn't.
The appointment auto-decline logic and real-time notifications system were also tricky to get right — making sure the timing, triggers, and status updates all fired correctly across different scenarios.
Clean URL routing via .htaccess gave me a headache during deployment — getting it to work properly on Hostinger's shared hosting environment took a lot of trial and error.
Packaging the system as an Android APK via Android Studio WebView was a challenge I didn't expect — dealing with mixed content issues, viewport scaling, and making sure the PWA behaved correctly on mobile devices required a lot of fine-tuning.
And of course, mobile responsiveness—refining the UI across so many breakpoints, making sure every modal, sidebar, and hamburger menu worked smoothly on both desktop and mobile—was a long and tedious but necessary process.

📝 Academic & Research Challenges
On the research side, crafting the Patient Satisfaction Questionnaires for both pre-test and post-test required careful wording to ensure the questions were valid, unbiased, and aligned with the study's objectives using the 4-point Likert scale.
Writing the Conclusions and Recommendations section meant I had to tie together all the data, the system's performance, and the users' feedback into a coherent academic narrative — which was harder than it sounds.
Formatting APA citations under Mondriaan Aura College's modified style was another layer of detail that required precision and consistency throughout the entire paper.

Outcomes

All the hard work, late nights, and countless debugging sessions paid off. The Gavina Noble Dental Clinic Management System was successfully deployed to production at gavinadental.online—a fully functional, real-world system that the clinic could actually use.
The system streamlined the clinic's day-to-day operations, giving staff a centralized platform to manage appointments, patient records, treatment plans, inventory, and communications—all in one place. Patients gained a convenient portal where they could register, book appointments, and track their dental history online.
The Android APK brought the system even further—making it accessible as a native-feeling mobile app without needing a separate codebase.
On the academic side, the research findings validated the system's effectiveness, with patient satisfaction data from the pre-test and post-test questionnaires showing a positive response to the digitized clinic experience.
And the biggest recognition of it all—the system earned the Best in Project Implementation award at the Capstone/Thesis defense at Mondriaan Aura College, a testament to the quality, completeness, and real-world applicability of everything I built.
It wasn't just a school project—it was a fully deployed, award-winning system that I'm proud to call the centerpiece of my portfolio.
